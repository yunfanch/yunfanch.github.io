01

Coral archives · Climate reconstruction

A longer view of Indo-Pacific interactions

How have connections between the Indian and Pacific Oceans changed over the Industrial Era?

I combine coral isotope records from Tanzania with a wider western Indian Ocean proxy network to reconstruct regional sea surface temperature. Comparing independent reconstruction methods helps assess the robustness of past variability and changing inter-basin relationships.

Coral δ¹⁸O · CPS · PCA / PCR · RegEM · CESM1 Last Millennium Ensemble

02

Climate model evaluation · Hydroclimate

Indian Ocean warming and the monsoon

Why does CESM2 struggle to reproduce observed ocean warming and rainfall change?

Using large-ensemble and Pacific pacemaker experiments, I examine how Pacific variability and Indian Ocean dynamics affect warming and the Indian summer monsoon. This work connects model biases in ocean processes to uncertainty in regional hydroclimate.

CESM2 · Large ensembles · Pacific pacemaker experiments

Published · Geophysical Research Letters, 2026Read the paper ↗
03

Paleoclimate · External forcing

Climate variability across centuries

How do natural forcing and internal variability shape the Indian Ocean over longer timescales?

I use a 387-year Diploastrea heliopora coral record from the Mafia Archipelago, Tanzania, to investigate decadal to multidecadal Indian Ocean sea surface temperature variability. ERA5 reanalysis and solar-forcing experiments from CESM1 and GISS help examine atmospheric and oceanic mechanisms linking solar forcing to regional climate.

Coral δ¹⁸O / δ¹³C · ERA5 · CESM1 · GISS solar-forcing experiments

04

Coral archives · Indian Ocean variability

Reconstructing tropical Indian Ocean climate

How well do coral archives capture regional climate variability beyond the instrumental record?

During my master’s research, I analyzed coral δ¹⁸O records from four representative tropical Indian Ocean sites—Kenya, Seychelles, Tanzania, and Mentawai—to reconstruct regional climate variability over 1880–1999. This work assessed how coral records from different parts of the basin reflect tropical Indian Ocean climate.

Coral δ¹⁸O · Proxy reconstruction · Regional climate variability

Published · Journal of Tropical Oceanography, 2022Read the paper ↗
05

Indo-Pacific interactions · Coral proxies

Indo-Pacific connections recorded in South China Sea corals

Can coral proxies record the Indo-western Pacific Ocean capacitor effect?

I investigated Indo-western Pacific Ocean capacitor events and their influence on the South China Sea using coral δ¹⁸O and Sr/Ca records. This project used coral geochemical archives to examine the regional imprint of ocean–atmosphere interactions linking the tropical Indian and Pacific Oceans.

Coral δ¹⁸O · Sr/Ca · Indo-western Pacific Ocean capacitor effect

Published · Palaeogeography, Palaeoclimatology, Palaeoecology, 2023Read the paper ↗
06

Ocean dynamics · Climate & ecology

Ocean processes in the Indo-Pacific Convergence Zone

How do ocean processes across scales connect climate variability and ecological change?

I contributed to a collaborative review synthesizing multi-scale ocean dynamical and environmental processes in the Indo-Pacific Convergence Zone. The work examined their climatic and ecological impacts, bringing together physical oceanography and evidence of environmental change in the region.

Literature synthesis · Multi-scale ocean dynamics · Climate–ecology connections

Published · Earth-Science Reviews, 2023Read the review ↗
