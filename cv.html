Research experience

08/2023–present

Indo-Pacific Climate Variability, Interactions, and Regional Impacts

Research Assistant, Paleoclimate and Common Era Lab, University of Michigan · Advisor: Julia Cole

Reconstruct western Indian Ocean sea surface temperature using CPS, PCA, PCR, and RegEM; investigate inter-basin variability with CESM1 Last Millennium Ensemble simulations; diagnose Indian Ocean warming and monsoon impacts using CESM2 large-ensemble and Pacific pacemaker experiments. Develop a 387-year coral record and examine solar-forcing mechanisms using ERA5, CESM1, and GISS.

09/2020–06/2023

Climate Variability and Climate Dynamics of the Tropical Oceans

Research Assistant, State Key Laboratory of Tropical Oceanography, South China Sea Institute of Oceanology, Chinese Academy of Sciences · Advisor: Yan Du

Analyzed coral δ¹⁸O records from four Indian Ocean sites over 1880–1999; investigated Indo-western Pacific Ocean capacitor events in South China Sea coral records; synthesized dynamical processes and climatic and ecological impacts in the Indo-Pacific Convergence Zone.

04/2021–06/2021

Numerical Simulation of Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation

Graduate Researcher, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences · Advisor: Tianjun Zhou

Analyzed AMOC responses to freshwater forcing and wind stress in LICOM3.0 sensitivity experiments.

03/2018–06/2019

Three-Dimensional Temperature–Salinity Structure from Satellite Observations

Project Technician, Hohai University · Advisor: Xidong Wang

Reconstructed subsurface temperature and salinity fields from satellite observations using an inversion method with vertical-gradient constraints.
